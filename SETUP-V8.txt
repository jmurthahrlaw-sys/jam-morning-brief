WHAT V8 CHANGES
- Legal freshness window defaults to 48 hours instead of 96.
- IMAP still searches 7 days so weekends/holidays can find the newest newsletter,
  but only the newest ACC Newsstand and newest ELINfonet issue are parsed.
- ACC ingestion focuses on the U.S. Employment & Labor section instead of global
  employment material and general newsletter sections.
- Newsletter article pages are fetched on a best-effort basis to resolve the
  underlying article publication date and source-page description. If this fails,
  the newsletter issue date is retained as a fallback.
- Legal stories already used in a recent JAM brief are stored in
  data/brief_history.json and filtered from later briefs.
- The daily workflow commits that small story-history file automatically.
- Legal target is 6–10 useful notes on an active day: usually 4–6 California and
  2–4 federal/Minnesota, with a hard maximum of 10.
- Duplicate California AI/TPS and similar repeated coverage is consolidated.
- Old/stale dated notes are deterministically removed after editorial selection.
- Legal prompt explicitly distinguishes pending legislation from enacted rules,
  avoids unsupported accommodation/termination duties, and keeps federal-sector
  EEOC procedures separate from private/federal-contractor rules.

NO NEW SECRETS ARE REQUIRED.
Existing IMAP_USER and IMAP_APP_PASSWORD continue to be used.
